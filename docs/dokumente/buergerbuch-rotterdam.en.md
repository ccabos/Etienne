## Transcription

> *"Etienne Cabos geb. te Caussade is poorter geedt. Present de Hoere, van Alphen en van der Does, Burgemeesters."*

**Translation:**
*"Etienne Cabos, born in Caussade, has taken the citizen's oath. Present were Messrs. van Alphen and van der Does, Mayors."*

---

## Description

On **May 24, 1780**, Etienne Cabos was officially registered as a citizen (Poorter) of the city of Rotterdam. Citizenship was an important prerequisite for operating a business in Rotterdam.

### What did citizenship mean?

Rotterdam citizenship (Poorterschap) granted:

- The right to run a business
- Access to the guilds
- Participation in civic life
- Protection under city laws

### The Witnesses

The presence of two mayors at the oath-taking ceremony was customary. They represented the city government and administered the oath.

### The Path to Citizenship

To become a citizen, one had to:

1. Provide proof of good character
2. Take the citizen's oath
3. Pay a fee

The certificate from the French Reformed Church in Isenburg likely served Etienne as proof of his good character.

---

## The New Residence: Vissersdijk

After receiving citizenship, the family settled at **Vissersdijk** and opened a **haberdashery shop** there. Vissersdijk was a street in the center of Rotterdam, near the harbor.

## Significance for Family History

This entry documents:
