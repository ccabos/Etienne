# Research Outlook: Open Questions and Avenues for Further Research

This family history rests on a remarkably dense body of sources - and yet a number of questions remain open. This page collects them and names, for each, a **concrete avenue for research**: which archive, which database, which search term. It is intended as a toolkit for anyone wishing to take this history further.

!!! info "Note on the online databases"
    Many of the portals named here are freely accessible and searchable, but often provide only **finding aids** (summaries), not digital images. A hit then means: the original file must be ordered in the relevant reading room or requested as a reproduction. A *non*-hit proves nothing as long as the cataloguing of the holdings is unfinished.

## 1. The Greatest Puzzle: The Brother Condemned "in Effigy"

The [Bulletin of 1907](bulletin-1907.md) reports that Etienne's **older brother** was *"effigié"* in Caussade - **executed in effigy**, i.e. condemned in absentia because he had **fled**. Who was he?

### Three Hypotheses

| # | Hypothesis | For | Against |
|---|------------|-----|---------|
| **A** | **Another, older brother** (born before 1737, hitherto unknown) | The Bulletin sentence explicitly separates Etienne and the convict ("dont le frère aîné"); the information probably came from the well-informed Thomas Thuet of Caussade | No such brother is documented in the church registers - he would need his own baptismal entry |
| **B** | **Etienne himself** - condemned in absentia as a deserter from the French army; the story shifted onto a "brother" in the retelling | Etienne is the family's only documented fugitive; the Bulletin explicitly introduces him as a "déserteur"; desertion was a capital crime punished in effigy; needs no unknown fifth son (Occam's razor) | Contradicts the wording, which separates Etienne and the brother |
| **C** | The younger brother **Pierre** (b. 1740), whose trail is lost after his baptism - assuming a single error by the author (on "aîné"/older) | Pierre's later fate is entirely unknown | Pierre was younger, not older than Etienne |

### Where to Look

- **Judgment registers of the Parlement de Toulouse (Série B)** - this is where the actual condemnation in absentia and its execution "in effigy" would be recorded. These registers are largely **not yet digitized** and must be consulted in the reading room in Toulouse.
- **[Sacs à procès of the Parlement de Toulouse](https://archives.haute-garonne.fr/n/les-sacs-a-proces-du-parlement-de-toulouse/n:110)** (case-file finding aid, online): search by name in the *"parties"* field for **Cabos** (also variants *Cabosse, Cabau, Cabot*), *"lieux des faits"* = **Caussade**, period **1758-1765**. Also filterable directly as open data at [data.haute-garonne.fr](https://data.haute-garonne.fr/explore/dataset/sacs-a-proces-du-parlement-de-toulouse-lot-02/table/). *Note:* only ~14,000-15,000 cases have been catalogued so far; the database is incomplete.
- **French army desertion lists** (for hypothesis B): [Service historique de la Défense, Vincennes](https://www.servicehistorique.sga.defense.gouv.fr/) - a deserter "Etienne Cabos", born ~1737 in Caussade, would be traceable there with regiment and date.
- **Caussade church registers before 1737** (for hypothesis A): is there another son of Laurens and Marie (b. ~1730-1736)? [Archives départementales de Tarn-et-Garonne](https://archivesdepartementales.ledepartement82.fr/).

## 2. Etienne's Vanished Years (ca. 1757-1772)

Between the baptism of 1737 and his appearance in Stettin in 1772 lie 35 almost undocumented years - in particular the gap between the [Rochette Affair](bulletin-1907.md) (1761/62, Etienne was 24) and the marriage in Stettin. Somewhere in this period lies the unknown path from southern France into the Prussian army.

**Avenues:**

- **Prussian military church registers and regimental rolls**: the Geheimes Staatsarchiv Preußischer Kulturbesitz (Berlin) holds military church registers; they were filmed by the Mormons and are partly online via **Ancestry**. A muster or recruitment entry could date Etienne's entry into Infantry Regiment No. 8.
- **Regiment "de Brévern"/"von Bevern"**: check whether Cabos served for a time in the [v. Bevern regiment](kriegsende-1779.md) (the Bulletin says "de Brévern") - via the Stettin garrison rolls.
- **French military service before the desertion**: see Vincennes (point 1).

## 3. The Fate of the Lost Children

The [death entry of the mother (1810)](sterbeurkunde-justine-1810.md) names a **son missing since ~1790** - it must have been **Johann Carl Abraham** (b. 1772) or **Franz Alexander** (b. 1776); the other of the two died before 1810 undocumented. **Henriette Charlotte** (b. 1777) also disappears early (in 1807 Elisabeth counts as the "only daughter").

**Avenues:**

- **Rotterdam DTB registers (until 1792)** via [Open Archives](https://www.openarchieven.nl/) and the [Stadsarchief Rotterdam](https://stadsarchief.rotterdam.nl/): possible burial entries for Henriette or one of the sons. The [baptism entry of Etienne jr. (1783)](https://www.openarchieven.nl/srt:D824CB9E-6282-409D-8CCC-ED38ADA69D89) is already recorded there - the family's other entries should be findable.
- **Berlin/Charlottenburg church registers (from 1792)**: death entries of the siblings who died in Berlin.
- **Maritime and military sources** for the missing son: a young man who vanished from Rotterdam around 1790 may have gone to sea or become a soldier (VOC/navy registers in the Dutch archives).

## 4. The Generation of Parents and Siblings in Caussade

- **Baptismal entry of the sister [Anne](hochzeit-anne-1771.md)** (~1742) and a possible **further brother** before 1737: Caussade church registers, [AD Tarn-et-Garonne](https://archivesdepartementales.ledepartement82.fr/).
- **Burial of the father Laurens Cabos** (died [between 1760 and 1771](hochzeit-anne-1771.md)): likewise Caussade.
- **The Lacombe-Cabos degree of kinship**: the [dispensation of 1771](hochzeit-anne-1771.md) proves consanguinity in the third degree - through whom? The answer could open up further branches of the family.
- **Descendants of Anne and Pierre Lacombe**: notarial and church registers of Réalville/Caussade.

## 5. Etienne's Berlin and Halle Years

- **Berlin address books (from 1799)**: fully digitized at the [Zentral- und Landesbibliothek Berlin](https://digital.zlb.de/). Etienne should appear there as *"Dentiste"*/dentist in the volumes ca. 1799-1808 - **the chance to find his Berlin address.**
- **Original dental advertisements**: the ULB Saxony-Anhalt is digitizing the **Wöchentliche Hallische Anzeigen (1731-1810)**. They can be searched via the [ULB "Historical Newspapers" portal](https://digital.bibliothek.uni-halle.de/hz) and - with full-text search - via the [German Newspaper Portal (DDB)](https://www.deutsche-digitale-bibliothek.de/newspaper), where one could search directly for "Cabos". *Whether exactly the volumes for 1794 and 1798 are already online remains to be verified.*
- **The intermediate stay in (Neu-)Isenburg**: church registers of the French Reformed congregation of [Neu-Isenburg](reisepass-1780.md) could confirm the family's stay in 1779/80.
