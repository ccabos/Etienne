## Transcription (Excerpt)

The long entry is partly difficult to decipher. The essential passages read:

> *"L'an mille sept cent soixante onze et le deux[ième] jour du mois d'octobre dans l'église paroissiale de St Jean de Réalville [...] entre Mr Pierre Lacombe agé d'environ trente cinq ans, fils légitime et naturel de Mr Antoine Lacombe [...] royal de la ville de Caussade et de demoiselle Marthe Lafon mariés [...] de la d[ite] ville de Caussade d'une part; et l'autre demoiselle Anne Cabos, fille légitime et naturelle de **feu** Sr Laurent Cabos bourg[eois] et de demoiselle Marie Rey, agée d'environ vingt neuf ans [...]"*

*In the year 1771, on the second day of the month of October, in the parish church of Saint-Jean de Réalville [...] between Mr Pierre Lacombe, about 35 years old, legitimate and natural son of Mr Antoine Lacombe [...] of the town of Caussade and Demoiselle Marthe Lafon, married couple of the said town of Caussade, on the one hand; and on the other hand Demoiselle Anne Cabos, legitimate and natural daughter of the **deceased** Sr Laurent Cabos, bourgeois, and Demoiselle Marie Rey, about 29 years old [...]*

The remainder of the text records:

- The threefold publication of the banns without objection
- An **impediment of consanguinity in the third degree** ("empêchement dirimant du troisième au troisième degré de consanguinité"), lifted by a **dispensation from the Bishop of Cahors** ("Monseigneur l'Evêque de Cahors")
- A **marriage contract**, drawn up by notary Larrieu in Réalville
- As witnesses, among others, Géraud Lacombe, cousin of the groom

## Significance: Anne Cabos - Etienne's Sister

!!! warning "Important discovery"
    This document proves that Etienne Cabos also had a **sister named Anne** - and it incidentally provides the only known bracketing of the death date of his father Laurens Cabos!

### What We Know About Anne

| Fact | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Name** | Anne Cabos |
| **Birth** | ca. 1742 (calculated from the age given as "about 29 years") |
| **Parents** | Laurens Cabos and Marie Rey (identical with Etienne's parents) |
| **Marriage** | October 2, 1771 in Saint-Jean de Réalville |
| **Husband** | Pierre Lacombe (ca. 1736), son of Antoine Lacombe of Caussade |

### The Father Was Already Dead in 1771

The entry describes Anne as the daughter of **"feu Sr Laurent Cabos"** - the *deceased* Mr Laurent Cabos. This allows the death date of Etienne's father to be bracketed for the first time:

- **February 1760**: Laurens is still alive - he releases his son Jean from paternal authority in the [marriage contract](hochzeit-jean-1760.md)
- **October 1771**: Laurens is dead

**Laurens Cabos therefore died between February 1760 and October 1771.** The temporal proximity to Etienne's emigration is remarkable: only nine months after Anne's wedding, in July 1772, Etienne married in Stettin. Whether the death of the father (and a possible division of the inheritance) triggered the emigration is not documented - but it is an obvious conjecture.

### The Connection to the Lacombe Family

The Lacombe family was evidently closely intertwined with the Cabos family:

- Already at the [marriage contract of brother Jean (1760)](hochzeit-jean-1760.md), **Pierre Lacombe** (practitioner) and **Laurent Teulières** were witnesses as *cousins of the groom* - and the marriage contract itself was drawn up by a **notary Lacombe**
- The dispensation for **consanguinity in the third degree** confirms: Pierre Lacombe and Anne Cabos were related to each other

Marriage within the extended family was not unusual among the bourgeoisie of small towns - it kept property and business relations within the family.

### A Catholic Wedding

Unlike brother Jean, who was married in a Protestant ceremony "in the Désert" (underground) in 1760, Anne married in 1771 in a **Catholic parish church** with episcopal dispensation - another example of how the Cabos family maneuvered between enforced Catholic conformity and Protestant tradition.
