## Transcription

### French Original (partial)

> *"Pierre Cabos fils du Sieur Laurens Cabos marchant et de Damoiselle marie Rely [Rey] de la présente ville et né le premier novembre mil sept cens quarante a esté ce dit le [jour] baptisé... parrain... Jean... Delpech... marraine Jeanne Delpech..."*

### English Translation

Pierre Cabos, son of Sieur **Laurens Cabos**, merchant, and Demoiselle **Marie Rey**, of this town [Caussade], born on **the first of November seventeen hundred and forty** [November 1, 1740], was baptized on the same day. Godfather: Jean Delpech (?), Godmother: Jeanne Delpech (?).

## Significance: Pierre - The Third Brother

!!! info "Important Discovery"
    With Pierre, we have now identified the **third son** of the Cabos family!

### The Sons of Laurens Cabos and Marie Rey

| Name | Birth Year | Age Relative to Etienne | Fate |
|------|-------------|--------------------------|-----------|
| **Jean Cabos** | before 1737? | Older? | †1796 Caussade |
| **Etienne Cabos** | 1737 | - | †1808 Berlin |
| **Pierre Cabos** | 1740 | 3 years younger | ? |

### Was Pierre the "Executed Brother"?

The [Bulletin from 1907](bulletin-1907.md) speaks of Etienne's **"older brother"** who was executed.

**Pierre CANNOT have been the one**, because:
- Pierre was born in **1740**
- Etienne was born in **1737**
- Pierre was therefore **3 years younger** than Etienne, not older

### Consequence for the Research

If the 1907 Bulletin is correct:
- Jean (†1796) was not the one - he died peacefully
- Pierre was not the one - he was younger, not older
- There must have been a **fourth brother** who was born **before 1737**!

!!! question "Open Questions"
    - **What became of Pierre?** Are there further documents about his life?
    - **Fourth brother?** Was there an older brother who was born before Etienne (1737)?
    - **Godparents Delpech**: Who were Jean and Jeanne Delpech? Relatives?

## The Delpech Family

The godparents **Jean Delpech** and **Jeanne Delpech** are interesting:
- The name Delpech also appears in other entries on the same church register page
- Possibly a related family or close friends of the Cabos family
- The name suggests an established family among the bourgeoisie of Caussade
